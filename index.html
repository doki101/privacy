MockNavi – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

MockNavi ("the App") is an Android application that provides GPS mock location simulation for developers and testers working on navigation and map-based applications. This document explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Data we do NOT collect

MockNavi has no user account or login system, and does not send any personal data to a remote server. All simulation settings, routes, and position data are stored locally on the user's own device only.

2. Permissions requested by the App

  • Location / mock location (ALLOW_MOCK_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): required so the App can feed a simulated GPS position to the Android system and to other apps for testing purposes. This position data never leaves the device and is not stored or transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, nearby devices): an optional feature that lets the App send the simulated position as NMEA data to a Bluetooth device chosen by the user. This connection occurs directly between the device and the paired accessory.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): required to show and control the status of the background simulation service.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the Google Maps SDK for map display, which may process certain technical device data when loading map tiles, independently of the developer, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

In-app purchases are processed through the Samsung Galaxy Store / Google Play billing system; the developer of MockNavi does not see or store any payment information.

4. Data sharing

MockNavi does not share or sell user data to any third party.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from users under the age of 13.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, with the effective date updated accordingly.
